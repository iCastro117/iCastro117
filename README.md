## About Me

Systems engineering professional with a strong passion for **UX/UI design** and the creation of user-centered digital experiences. I design interfaces that are accessible, consistent and measurable, validating every decision with real users before shipping — and I prototype in **Figma**, then bring designs to life with **HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Java**.

Curious by nature, I combine systems engineering with a designer's eye to solve real problems in Latin America — from UX case studies to freelance AI-training work evaluating and improving large language models.
